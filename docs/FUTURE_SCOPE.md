# Future scope

Potential extensions, not implemented in the academic scope:

- AI travel assistant, personalized recommendations, recommendation engine and AI itinerary generation.
- Weather and maps integrations, reviews and ratings.
- Email notifications and password recovery, QR booking tickets, PDF invoices.
- Coupons, multiple languages and advanced analytics.
- Real payment gateway and refund reconciliation.
- External travel APIs and actual inventory/supplier allocation, with explicit transactional and operational guarantees.

Each extension requires separate requirements, threat modeling, data/privacy decisions and testing. The present application intentionally books complete predefined demo packages.
